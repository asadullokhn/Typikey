The board follows the sentence again.

Tapping "I" used to change the word keys around it — "go" became "going", "be" became "am" — without any key moving. That stopped working, and the board looked normal while it happened, so there was nothing to see except that it had gone quiet.

The cause was the app, not the keyboard. Opening the board editor and saving anything at all — adding a page, renaming one — wrote a copy of the shipped home board into the shared container, and the keyboard read that copy as a board somebody had arranged by hand. It stops rearranging those, on purpose. It just could not tell the difference between a board that was arranged and a copy of its own.

Devices carrying one of those copies recover on this update. Nothing to reset.

Boards you really did arrange now relabel their verbs in place too, which they should have done all along.

Please try: tap "I", then ABC and type "am", then Home — "go" should read "going", in the same place it was. Tap "you" and "be" should read "are". Then arrange a board of your own and check the same thing still happens on it.
